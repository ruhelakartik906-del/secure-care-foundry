# Unicare Medical Solutions — Build Plan

This is a large build, so it ships in 4 phases. Each phase leaves you with a working site.

## Phase 1 — Public website and design
- Look: deep navy and medical blue, teal accent, white and light grey surfaces, square-edged cards, Manrope headings with a matching body font, very little animation. Your logo goes in the header and footer.
- Sticky header with a compact Products dropdown (9 products), a mobile menu, and a "Get a Quote" button.
- Homepage built in the exact 15-section order you gave.
- Products listing with category filters, plus a page for each product. The Modular OT page gets the full long-form layout.
- About, Contact (map, WhatsApp, phone), Blog list and post pages, Privacy, Disclaimer, Terms, an HTML Sitemap page, and a 404 page.
- Realistic product photos generated for OT, gas pipeline, CSSD and the other products. No doctor stock photos.
- Sticky WhatsApp and "Get Quote" buttons on mobile.
- All company facts are left as clearly marked placeholders: address, phone, certifications, years, client counts. No fake prices; products show "Price available on request".

## Phase 2 — Lovable Cloud: enquiries and admin
- Turn on Lovable Cloud for the database, file uploads and admin sign-in.
- The enquiry form and the consultation form save to the database, including file uploads, and then show the thank-you message.
- Admin panel: dashboard and enquiries (search, filter, change status through New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal Sent, Converted and Closed, CSV export, delete).
- Admins are kept in a separate roles table.

## Phase 3 — Content management
- In the admin panel, manage products (price type: starting price, price range, or on request), categories, blogs (rich editor, draft/publish/schedule, SEO fields, FAQ), testimonials (published or draft), projects with images, and contact and site settings.
- The public pages read everything from the database, so new blogs show up on the homepage, the blog page and the sitemap automatically.

## Phase 4 — Location pages and SEO
- Product + state location pages for every Indian state and union territory. Each page has its own content and a city list, and admins control Published, Noindex or Draft. Only pages with real content are published.
- A footer "Service Locations" list that can be collapsed.
- Site search across products, blogs and locations.
- XML sitemap, robots.txt, and structured data for the organization, breadcrumbs, articles, FAQs (only where real FAQs exist) and products.
- Ready-made connection points for Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel, with tracking for quote, phone and WhatsApp clicks and form submissions.
- Settings for email notifications.

## What I need from you, whenever you can
Company address, phone and WhatsApp numbers, email, business hours, the admin login email, and any real certifications, testimonials or project photos.
